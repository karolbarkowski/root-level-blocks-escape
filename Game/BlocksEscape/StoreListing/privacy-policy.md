# Privacy Policy for Out of the Barn

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Out of the Barn ("the game") is made by RootLevel.It. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

## The short version

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It works fully offline.

## Information the game keeps

The game saves your progress (which levels you have solved, the last level you played) and your settings (music and sound on or off) **on your device only**. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Information we do not collect

- No account, sign-in, name, email address or contact details
- No location
- No advertising ID; the game shows no ads
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No in-app purchases or payment information
- No access to your photos, files, contacts, camera or microphone

## Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. It is distributed through Google Play, which handles downloads and updates under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

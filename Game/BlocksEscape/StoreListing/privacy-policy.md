# Privacy Policy for Out of the Barn

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Out of the Barn ("the game") is made by RootLevel.It. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

## The short version

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. The first section of levels is free; the rest can be unlocked with a single in-app purchase, which is handled entirely by Google Play. The game can be played offline.

## Information the game keeps

The game saves your progress (which levels you have solved, the last level you played), your settings (music and sound on or off) and whether you have unlocked all levels **on your device only**. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## In-app purchase

The game offers one optional, one-time purchase that unlocks all levels. The purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Google Play terms of service. Your payment details are entered in Google Play and are never seen or stored by the game or by us.

When the game starts, it asks Google Play on your device whether your Google account already owns the unlock, so that a purchase comes back after a reinstall or on another device. The game only receives a yes/no answer and the price to display; it does not receive your name, email address or payment information. We do not run any server of our own.

## Information we do not collect

- No account, sign-in, name, email address or contact details
- No payment or card information (purchases are handled by Google Play)
- No location
- No advertising ID; the game shows no ads
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No access to your photos, files, contacts, camera or microphone

## Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. It is distributed through Google Play, which handles downloads, updates and the in-app purchase under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases using Google Play's parental controls.

## Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

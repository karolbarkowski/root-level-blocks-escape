## Full description (4000 characters max)

The animals want out of the barnyard pen, and every gate only opens for its own kind. Can you lead them all home?

Out of the Barn is a relaxing sliding-block puzzle set on a sunny meadow. Each level is a fenced pen packed with chubby animal blocks: cows, pigs, sheep, chickens, horses, cats and dogs. Drag a block anywhere it can slide; drop it next to a gate of the same animal and it trots out of the pen. Clear every animal to solve the level.

It sounds simple, until the pen gets crowded. Plan your moves, make room in the right order and find the way out for everyone.

FEATURES
• 1,200 levels, from gentle warm-ups to brain-twisting pens
• Six areas to explore: Meadow, Orchard, Pond, Barnyard, Hayloft and Hilltop
• Every level is solvable, verified by a solver before it reached the game
• New twists as you go: sleepy animals that wake up once others have left, animals that only slide one way, stubborn hay bales that never leave, and double animals that need two different gates
• Pick any level at any time: no locks, no waiting
• Cute 3D animals and a cozy farm to tap around in (try the butterfly!)
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no internet connection needed
• Your progress is saved on your device

Perfect for a quick puzzle break or a long relaxing session. The animals are waiting!
